We are building AI-native data infrastructure for the full building lifecycle — transforming fragmented building data into computable, auditable, and continuously appreciating assets.

Built on 20+ years of architecture and BIM expertise combined with an AI-native data architecture, we deliver solutions for energy optimization, carbon asset management, and lifecycle data management. Starting from Asia-Pacific, we are building the data foundation for the global built environment.

What We Do

The building industry is shifting from delivery-oriented to performance-oriented — yet building data remains fragmented across contractors, consultants, and operators, non-computable and non-auditable. Our work is to change this. Through AI, we integrate and structure BIM and all building data, establishing standardized data models that continuously generate specialized databases for energy optimization, carbon accounting, and lifecycle analysis — providing a directly callable data foundation for building asset operations and decision-making.

Our five solutions span the full building lifecycle — low-carbon materials, construction decarbonization, building operations, circular retrofit and deconstruction, and carbon asset compliance — delivering scalable, data-driven tools to developers, asset managers, contractors, and operators, transforming building data into continuously appreciating carbon assets and maximizing data value.

Energy is the entry point. Data infrastructure is the business.
